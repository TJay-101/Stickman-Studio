How to make your video

1. Tap Record video and let the scene play through. 2. Tap Stop & save. 3. Your browser will download a video file (WebM). You can keep it on your device and upload it to YouTube. This first version uses generated visuals and has no voice narration yet.

Tip: Keep this page open while recording. If your browser asks for permission, allow downloads.
